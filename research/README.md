# research

Working notes behind the skill. Everything in `skills/` is the polished
output; this is the evidence, including the parts that did not survive.

Read in this order if you are new to it.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `research-pass-3.md` | The third research pass. Firsthand iOS growth numbers, with the fabrications traced and the four corrections it forced on the skill |
| `bier-tactical-hacks.md` | The operator's compressed playbook: onboarding, paywalls, viral loop, iOS hacks, launch. Plus a provenance note on which figures are actually attributable |
| `explode-teardown.md` | One page on the app where most of those tactics shipped together, including which ones breach Apple's guidelines and which do not |
| `growth-tips-catalog.md` | Tips with numbers, tagged Network / Utility / Universal, and a section on which of them are actively harmful applied to a utility |
| `x-growth-tips.md` | The X posts behind the catalogue, indexed with verified permalinks and the method for checking them logged out |
| `research-pass-3-x-notes.md` | The chase list. Claims attributed to an operator that could not be traced to anything he published |
| `NEXT-RESEARCH-PROMPT.md` | Self-contained brief for the next pass. Covers Network, Utility and Hybrid, and lists the five gaps that have survived three passes |

## The two rules this folder runs on

**Claim, number, URL, or it does not go in.** A claim without a number is
not a finding. A number without a working URL is not a finding either.

**"Nothing found" is publishable.** Three of the skill's most useful
passages exist because somebody went looking, found nothing, and wrote
that down instead of quoting something invented. The still-empty gaps are
listed at the bottom of `research-pass-3.md` and in the skill's
`what-nobody-has-measured.md`.

## One thing worth knowing about scope

The skill began as a playbook for apps that are *not* viral teen social
apps, because that shape is the one the published literature already
covers. The research now deliberately covers **all** shapes, because the
comparison is what makes either half usable: the tactics only make sense
once you can see which shape they were measured on, and which ones break
when carried across. `growth-tips-catalog.md` is where that comparison
lives.
