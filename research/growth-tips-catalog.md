# Growth tips catalogue, tagged by app shape

Every tip here carries a number, a gate, a threshold or a count, and a
URL. Soft advice is omitted, on the same principle as
`bier-tactical-hacks.md`: a tip you cannot check against a measurement
is a slogan.

Sources are mostly Nikita Bier, because he is the only operator in this
field who publishes thresholds rather than sentiment. That is also the
problem: **his numbers come from dense-network teen social apps, and most
of them are wrong or harmful for a utility.** Hence the tagging.

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **[N]** | Network. Dense-graph social. Its native habitat |
| **[U]** | Utility. Solo value, first open, no other people required |
| **[★]** | Universal. True regardless of shape |

The section at the bottom, **"actively harmful on a utility"**, is the
most useful part of this file and the reason it exists.

## Universal [★]

### Funnel and permission gates

| Tip | Number | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do not call a zero-to-one launch a failure until push approval clears a floor | "don't declare it a failure until your **push notification approval rate is higher than 50%**. Anything lower is likely distorting your data" | [Bier, 10 Oct 2019](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1182338827215503360) |
| Push approval is a solved problem. Copy, do not invent | "There are proven ways to get **85% push notification approval**" | [Bier, 13 Mar 2025](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1900034795775955120) |
| Gate the launch on store-page conversion before you gate it on anything else | Test screenshot conversion, **minimum 20% to proceed with launch**. Also: audit open-graph images on all links, verify invites deliver on all carriers | [Bier, 8 Jul 2021](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1413035791941607429) |
| Peer influence is about half the adoption decision, not all of it | "**49.9% of a user's decision to adopt is informed by their peers**" | [Bier, 22 Sep 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1837913699723706390) |

### Time to value

| Tip | Number | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invert time to value. The aha moment measured in seconds, not screens | "if you can't demonstrate value in the **first three seconds**, it's over" | [Bier, 18 Jul 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1813735668276928682) · [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |
| Three exposures before a download, so saturate one area rather than spreading | "to be convinced to download an app you need to see the marketing message **three times or so**" | [Lenny's Podcast, 25 Aug 2024](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |

### Design and onboarding

| Tip | Number | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Never build a user tour. Fix the four things instead | Fix **4 things: navigation, hierarchy and layout, empty states, copywriting** | [Bier, 7 Aug 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1821223122261139456) |
| Big obvious changes beat funnel forensics | "A dumb PM will stare at an onboarding funnel for hours and ultimately improve conversion by **5%**. A smart PM will look at the funnel in a minute, make a button bigger, and improve conversion by **20%**" | [Bier, 13 Aug 2021](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1426229686175027201) |
| Every place you fragment the product multiplies your failure risk | "expect to **increase your app's overall probability of failure by 50%**" per fragmented part | [Bier, 25 Jan 2023](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1618294904538685446) |
| Do not innovate on commodity mechanics | "A blockbuster app does **ONE** thing totally different. Everything else, like invites and friendfinding? Copy what the old companies did 10 years ago" | [Bier, 22 Jan 2021](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1352749766204002304) |

### Testing discipline

| Tip | Number | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Put a hard clock and a hard number on each attempt | "**Development time cannot exceed 3 weeks. Revenue must be a minimum of $3 million in 90 days.** I've counted over 20 apps in the App Store that have achieved this" | [Bier, 5 Jul 2020](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1279826442830807041) |
| Validate the chain in order, not the whole product at once | "1. Users need to share the app X number times 2. The shares then need to convert to downloads at X rate 3. Then, X% of users need to pay" | [Bier, 5 Jun 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1798372438960730423) |
| Test the ideal, not the MVP, and remove confounders | Run **24-hour in-app live chat support** during validation, both to unblock users and as the research channel | [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |
| Murderboard before launch | List every possible criticism with the worst possible headline, then reposition. Do not delegate it | [Bier, 7 Jan 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1744091716423176433) |
| Incumbents are slow, and you can price that in | Big companies take "**about 12 to 24 months** to respond to competitive threats" | [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |
| Consumer is random even when you are good at it | Of the companies he advises, "**50% of the time it's outright failure** because consumer is so random" | [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |

### Distribution mechanics

| Tip | Number | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bulk SMS with a link needs a warmed number | "You cannot send bulk texts containing a URL until your number has earned credibility with phone carriers. To earn credibility, it needs to have replies from other numbers" | [Bier, 12 Dec 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1867029883387580571) |

## Utility [U]

The short list, and the reason this file exists. These are the only four
places Bier states a method for a **solo-value** product, and all four
are the same move: **manufacture a shareable artefact, or intercept an
existing recurring behaviour. Do not try to build a graph.**

That is the same conclusion `in-app-growth.md` reaches from the opposite
direction, which is the strongest cross-check either of them gets.

| Tip | Number | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Collapse the product to one memorable action.** Dupe replaced a multi-feature shopping app with "prepend `dupe.com` to any product URL", taking time to value "from minutes to near zero seconds" | Reached **millions in ARR within 60 days**; the quoted post cites **2M+ users** | [Bier, 18 Jul 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1813735668276928682) · [Levin, 17 Jul 2024](https://x.com/iamjasonlevin/status/1813691788475195868) |
| **Intercept the behaviour instead of owning the session.** VYBE went viral then churned in days. The fix: find the one retained sub-behaviour (trying on clothes), rebuild around it, then ship a **mobile Safari extension** that fires on any shopping page | "**Retention nearly 4x.** Once the user enabled the extension, they were essentially retained for life." Monetisation moved from a cancellable subscription to a **background commission on every purchase** | [Bier, 10 Apr 2025](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1910401111107715273) |
| **Bolt a shareable artefact onto a solo tool.** Death Clock: "Health apps are notoriously difficult to grow: they are **single-player utilities** that resonate most with older audiences." The fix was "a **shareable moment** that made the value proposition tangible", a survey predicting your death date and projecting how you would age | "drove the **cost of acquisition down to pennies**" (no absolute figure) | [Bier, 22 Jan 2025](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1882152092221222946) · [part 2](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1882152097786761517) |
| **The generic single-player recipe he states.** "Four months of grinding, but finally unlocked some virality for a single-player app. It worked by (1) **renaming the app to something with a stronger hook** and (2) **generating shareable content during the signup flow**" | Four months. No conversion figure | [Bier, 4 Dec 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1864386996522643827) |

Note the renaming tip lands on exactly the same advice as the skill's
search stack: **the name is a keyword field and a hook, not a brand
exercise.** HabitKit is the measured version of the same idea, named
"Habit Tracker - HabitKit" with 98% of its users arriving from store
search. See `research-pass-3.md`.

## Network [N]

Included so it can be recognised and *not* applied. These are the
thresholds that make Bier's playbook work, and every one of them assumes
value is a function of who else is present.

| Tip | Number | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contacts permission is the whole business, or you do not have one | "if **less than 70% of your users are granting access to contacts, your app is dead on arrival** until that's fixed" and users have "a **5 second attention span**" | [Bier, 11 Jun 2021](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1403498766737444865) |
| The contacts benchmark, and the iOS 18 cliff | The contacts screen "averages about **65% approval** across all apps. It's higher for teens, lower for adults." Then iOS 18 added per-contact selection: "as of iOS 18, friend-based contact sync apps are basically **dead on arrival**" | [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) · [Bier, 19 Sep 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1836612494938509664) |
| Local density is the real threshold, and it is 10% | "density in one specific area (**approx. 10% penetration to get the flywheel spinning**)" | [Bier, 27 Jun 2021](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1409238896039260162) |
| Invite propensity collapses with age | "Number of invitations sent per user **drops 20% for every additional year of age, from 13 years old to 18**. So if you build for adults, expect to pay to acquire every user with ads" | [Bier, 23 Sep 2022](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1573365055802036224) |
| The age ceiling, in his words | "If you're building a social app targeted at **people over 25**, you are on a death march" | [Bier, 11 Dec 2020](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1337464195667742720) |
| Escape velocity, expressed as sessions not K-factor | "**each app session should trigger 7 new people to open your app**" | [Bier, 12 Jan 2022](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1481118424503775233) |
| Conditions for value must be orchestrable | "Getting **7 adult friends** to install an app on a reproducible basis is non-trivial. If you can figure out how to do that, that's a bigger idea than your original concept" | [Bier, 12 Jan 2022](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1481118408766754818) |
| If the aha requires a crowd, it will never arrive | "if your product is only magical when you reach **40 friends**, good luck with that" | [Bier, 13 Feb 2020](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1227765328651812864) |
| A longer signup can raise completion if the extra step signals other people | Adding "use location to find nearby schools, pick your school" upfront **increased signup completion by more than 10%**: "it made the user feel like their people were on this app" | [Bier, 27 Jun 2020](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1276925368071409664) |
| Suggested-friends ranking, his stated default | **(1) by number of shared phone numbers, then alphabetical (2) friends of friends (3) from your school.** "People You Should Invite" ranks by number of app users who have that person in their contacts | [Bier, 27 Jun 2020](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1276694691744935936) |
| Launch, tested not scaled | tbh seeded one Georgia high school, chosen for the earliest US start date: "**40% of the school downloaded it in the first 24 hours**" | [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |
| Engagement that signals a real network | One tbh school: "**450,000 messages in the first seven days**." Day-one messaging on a typical messaging app is "three or four" per user; tbh was "like **60**" | [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |
| Throttle growth deliberately to protect the graph | State-by-state geofenced rollout so servers could scale. Product-market fit signature: "things breaking every three days" | [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |
| Icon and name gate invite rate, not just install rate | tbh and the Gas predecessor "indexed about **60% to 65% women**"; boys would not invite friends to a pink app called Crush. Renamed Gas, black icon, flame: "the invite rate jumped" (no figure given) | [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |
| Buy answers before the window closes | The Gas prototype at one school cost "**$600 in server costs per day, with a userbase of only 800 people**", paid deliberately to get answers before the school year ended | [Bier, 6 Feb 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1754896706880127185) |
| Monetise the curiosity gap, not the utility | Gas **God Mode**: paid reveal of who voted for you, **$6.99/week**. Sensor Tower via TechCrunch: **7.4M installs, about $7M consumer spend** | [TechCrunch, 17 Jan 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/01/17/discord-acquires-gas-a-compliments-based-social-media-app-for-teens/) |
| What #1 in the App Store costs | Historically "**80 to 100,000 installs**"; now "some days it's up to like **300,000**, and that's per day." tbh peaked at **360,000 a day** | [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |
| Crisis response is a growth number too | A trafficking hoax at peak: "**3% of users deleted accounts today**", later driven down to **0.1%** | [Bier, 14 Oct 2022](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1580852745627529216) · [Lenny's](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-consistently-go-viral-nikita-bier) |

## Actively harmful on a utility

The single most valuable section here. Each of these is widely quoted as
general app-growth advice and each will cost a utility real money.

- **"70% contacts or dead on arrival", and the 65% benchmark.** A
  converter, tracker or scanner has no reason to ask for contacts at all.
  Importing this gate means adding a permission prompt with no payoff,
  which lowers activation and invites review friction. Bier himself
  declares the whole mechanism dead as of iOS 18.

- **The 10% local-penetration threshold, and 40% of a school.** Density
  thresholds exist because value is a function of who else is present. A
  utility's value is constant at n=1. Chasing geographic density spends
  the budget on a variable that does not move retention.

- **The age curves. This is the most copied and most misapplied Bier
  claim.** "Invites fall 20% per year of age" and "adults have no
  friends" are statements about **invitation propensity**, not about
  willingness to pay. Utilities generally monetise older users better.
  Followed literally, these rules steer a utility away from its
  best-paying cohort.

- **The 7-friends and 40-friends heuristics.** Adopting "each session must
  trigger 7 opens" as a KPI for a utility manufactures a vanity metric
  and pushes the team to bolt social features onto a solitary product.
  Nobody wants a feed in their scanner.

- **"Every calorie wasted on non-relevant content or single-player
  features will compound until the network evaporates"**
  ([Bier, 22 Aug 2025](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1958799724934570401)).
  Read literally by a utility team this is an instruction to destroy
  their own product. He is describing social networks. Note the direct
  tension with his own Dupe, VYBE and Death Clock advice above, where he
  does the exact opposite for solo-value apps. **That tension is the most
  useful thing in this catalogue**, and it is the clearest evidence that
  the tactics are shape-specific rather than general.

- **Deliberate geofenced throttling.** Suppressing demand to protect a
  graph's density is rational for a social app. For a utility it is lost
  revenue.

- **"Excessively long sign up flows are fine if it leads to higher
  activation rates"**
  ([Bier, 12 Jan 2022](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1481118415154675715)).
  Qualified by activation, and his own supporting evidence is a *social*
  framing effect ("their people were on this app"). A utility has no such
  effect, and the tip contradicts his own three-second rule. Treat as
  network-specific.
