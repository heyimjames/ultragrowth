# Chase list: what could not be traced to a source

The counterpart to `x-growth-tips.md`. That file is what was verified.
This is what was not, and it is the more useful of the two, because
almost every item below is currently circulating as a fact.

Scope of the search behind these negatives: **3,798 verified-live Nikita
Bier posts** recovered and individually resolved, plus the full Lenny's
Podcast transcript, plus the leaked tbh memo, plus the Explode teardowns.
"Not found" means not in that corpus. It does not mean never said, and
the corpus has a known hole: **35.5% of his archived posts are deleted**,
and 2022 and 2023 are thinly archived.

## Attributed to Bier, not traceable to Bier

These matter because several appear in `bier-tactical-hacks.md`, which is
recorded there as an operator's compressed playbook rather than as a
sourced document. Anything in this section should be used as a heuristic
and **never quoted with his name on it**.

| Claim in circulation | Status |
| --- | --- |
| **"~6 screens max" onboarding**, or any stated onboarding screen count | **Not found.** Zero matches across the corpus, the Lenny transcript, or the memo |
| **"SMS > email", every extra field costs 5 to 10% completion** | **Not found as a Bier claim.** The circulating version ("SMS verification outperforms email for students because iOS auto-fills the code") traces only to [synergylabs.co](https://www.synergylabs.co/blog/the-nikita-bier-playbook-reverse-engineering-the-psychology-of-viral-app-design), an agency marketing page that writes it **in its own voice** ("we've found that") under a Bier-branded headline. His only SMS post is about carrier deliverability for bulk links |
| **"Activation bar: aim ≥40% install to first meaningful action"** | **Not found.** He publishes permission-rate gates (50%, 60%, 65%, 70%, 85%) and a store-conversion gate (20%), and **no activation-rate target at all** |
| **"Pre-permission lifts approval 20 to 30%"**, and soft-asks as stated doctrine | **Not found.** He *ships* the pattern, in Explode, and publishes *outcome* gates. No post prescribes the priming pattern or quantifies its lift |
| **"Soft-allow to system allow often 80%+"** | **Not found.** The nearest real figure is his own "proven ways to get **85% push notification approval**", which is an end-to-end approval rate, not a soft-ask conversion rate. Different denominator |
| **"K = invites/user × invite CVR. Need K > 1"** | **Not found as a Bier threshold.** One passing mention of the term in six years, [with no number](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1427421269654315008). He consistently expresses virality as **sessions triggered** (7 new opens per session) or **local penetration** (10%), never as a K target. If you see "Bier says K > 1.x", it is invented |
| **"22+ means buy every user"** | **The 22 is not his.** His published thresholds are **over 25 is "a death march"**, "adults have no friends", and "if you build for adults, expect to pay to acquire every user with ads". The number in circulation does not match the source |
| **"40% of one dense community in 24h or kill"**, as a gate | **Misread, not invented.** 40% of a Georgia high school in the first 24 hours is a **reported outcome** at one school, not a threshold, target or kill criterion. His only stated density *threshold* is **about 10% local penetration** |
| **"~4pm public launch"** timing | **Real, but not from Bier.** The verbatim "Golden Launch Hour" five-step recipe, including "At 4:00PM when school gets out", comes from **tbh's internal Facebook memo leaked to BuzzFeed in 2018**, attributed to the founders collectively. [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/ryanmac/facebooks-teens-tbh-psychological-trick-memo). On Lenny's he mentions 4:00 PM only to describe watching installs light up a map after school let out. Indie Hackers, Alexander Jarvis and Synergy Labs all present it as his stated tactic without flagging the provenance |
| **"Zero ratings loses ~2/3 of conversions"**, as a Bier claim | **Not found in his corpus.** The figure is real and is independently sourced in this repo's `SKILL.md`; it is simply not his, and the same agency page above attributes it to him with no citation |
| **"Aha in <10 seconds" for App Clips** | **Not found** in any Explode source. His published figure is **three seconds**, for time to value generally, not a ten-second App Clip gate |
| **"50+ short videos/day across accounts"** | **Not traced.** Not located in the verified corpus |

**The pattern worth naming.** A single agency marketing page,
synergylabs.co, is the traceable origin of at least three numbers now
circulating as Bier's, each written in that page's own voice under his
name. This is the exact laundering mechanism
`numbers-that-are-not-real.md` documents for ASO statistics, operating on
tactical advice instead. It is worth assuming that any "Bier playbook"
listicle with round percentages and no permalinks is the same thing.

## Deleted or unrecoverable

- **The Explode post-mortem.** Appfigures writes "I came across this post
  from Nikita explaining why the launch failed", and that hyperlink
  resolves to [a different user's question](https://x.com/MattMolinar/status/1883288484263420336)
  ("Why didn't you test explode in high schools? @nikitabier", 25 January
  2025), not to a Bier post. The intended target is his reply in that
  conversation. **Best candidate: status `1883628269662240783`, 26
  January 2025, the only Bier post in that window present in the Wayback
  index, and now deleted.** Its single archived snapshot is a 1.9KB
  JavaScript shell containing no text. **Text not recoverable**, and the
  identification is unproven: X's guest conversation endpoint is shut
  down, so replies cannot be retrieved logged out.

  What *is* recoverable is his [4 February 2025 update](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1886856138735423763),
  which does not read as a post-mortem: "It was a piece of growth hacking
  art." Use that, and do not assert that he published a failure analysis.

- **Post 25 of the January 2022 thread.** Deleted. Text survives only in
  a ThreadReader unroll.

- **Scale, quantified:** of 5,908 archived status URLs, **2,095 (35.5%)
  are dead**, concentrated in 2020 to 2021 and again in January and
  February 2025.

## Numbers in circulation that do not match their source

- **Gas revenue.** The sourced figures are Sensor Tower via
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/01/17/discord-acquires-gas-a-compliments-based-social-media-app-for-teens/):
  **7.4M installs, about $7M consumer spend.** On Lenny's, Lenny asserts
  "$10 million or $11 million in sales, 10 million downloads" and Bier
  answers "yeah, it was a blowout success". **The $10 to 11M circulating
  as Bier's own figure is Lenny's, unchallenged.** Quote 7.4M and ~$7M.
- **The tbh cash-crisis anecdote exists in three incompatible versions.**
  YouTube captions of Lenny's: Amazon bill "12,000" against a bank
  balance of "150,000". A transcript of the same episode: "$120,000"
  against "$150,000". Bier's own 2025 post: "our bank account was at
  **$100k** yet our Amazon bill was **$200k**"
  ([source](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1906094401325265255)). **Do
  not cite a figure here at all.**
- **App count.** "15 different apps" (Lenny's, August 2024) against
  "**+40 apps** over the last decade"
  ([June 2024](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1798372438960730423)), two
  months apart.
- **Gas acquisition date.** Lightspeed's own bio says Discord acquired
  Gas in **2022**; TechCrunch dates the announcement to **17 January
  2023**.
- **Gas price point.** The **$6.99/week** God Mode figure is
  well-sourced journalism. It is **not** a Bier statement that could be
  located.

## What to do with this file

Two rules, both of which the repo already applies to statistics and
should apply to tactics:

1. **A tactic with a number and no permalink is a tactic with no
   number.** Keep the mechanism, drop the figure.
2. **Check the shape before the source.** Even the verified numbers in
   `growth-tips-catalog.md` are mostly measurements of dense-network teen
   social apps. Being real is necessary and not sufficient.
