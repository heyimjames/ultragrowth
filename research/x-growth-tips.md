# X growth tips, indexed from guest view

The tactical layer of app growth is not in blog posts. It is in X posts,
mostly by one person, mostly undated in the way people quote them, and a
third of it has been deleted. This file is the indexed, verified version.

`growth-tips-catalog.md` organises these by app shape. This file is the
index and the provenance: what was said, where, and whether the post is
still there.

## Method, so this can be redone

Everything below was verified **while logged out**, because that is the
only reproducible way to cite X:

- **Text, author, date and engagement** come from X's own public
  endpoints: the `publish.twitter.com` oEmbed API and the
  `cdn.syndication.twimg.com/tweet-result` endpoint. A post that returns
  no payload from the latter is deleted or tombstoned.
- **Dates were cross-checked** by decoding the snowflake timestamp
  embedded in each status ID, rather than trusting a rendered date.
- **Discovery** used the Wayback Machine CDX index over
  `twitter.com/nikitabier/status/*` and `x.com/nikitabier/status/*`.

Two limitations that matter. **X's guest search and conversation
endpoints are shut down**, so replies are not retrievable logged out,
which is why some replies below are cited from an archive rather than
live. And the corpus is limited to what the Wayback Machine happened to
archive: 2022 and 2023 are very thinly covered, so "not found" here means
"not in the verified corpus", not "never said".

## The deletion rate, which is the finding nobody mentions

Of **5,908** archived Bier status URLs recovered from the CDX index,
**3,798 resolve live and 2,095 are deleted or tombstoned. That is
35.5%.** Deletions cluster in 2020 to 2021 and again around January and
February 2025.

**Practical consequence: treat any uncited Bier quote from 2020 or 2021
as probably unverifiable.** A large fraction of the advice circulating
under his name cannot be checked against a source that still exists,
which is exactly the condition under which numbers drift.

**Post 24 is the one nobody quotes and the one this repo should.** The
author of the most-cited growth thread in the field explicitly tells you
to discount it, on the grounds that almost nobody has seen
product-market fit and survivors are unreliable narrators. That is the
same argument `numbers-that-are-not-real.md` makes about statistics,
made about advice.

## Post B: inverting time to value

**https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1813735668276928682**, 18 July 2024
00:40 UTC (17 July, 5:40 PM Pacific), 760 likes:

> Here's a window into how I approach growing products: inverting the
> time to value, so that the user experiences the Aha Moment in seconds.
>
> For the companies I advise, it works particularly well when the founder
> is a generational talent in marketing like @ghoshal

It quote-tweets Jason Levin,
**https://x.com/iamjasonlevin/status/1813691788475195868**, 17 July 2024
21:46 UTC, 747 likes:

> What's it like working with @nikitabier on growth? Dupe founder
> @ghoshal explains: "Nikita injected the first dose of virality into the
> product."

This is the single most important post for a non-social app, because
**the product it describes is a utility**. Dupe's mechanic is prepending
`dupe.com` to a product URL, which is not an invitation and not a graph.
It is the artefact loop with the time to value cut to nearly zero. See
the Utility section of `growth-tips-catalog.md`.

Levin's handle is **@iamjasonlevin**. It is frequently miscited.
